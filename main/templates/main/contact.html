FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does it take to build a website?

The timeline depends on the complexity of your project. Simple websites typically take 1-2 weeks, while e-commerce sites and custom applications may take 3-6 weeks. We'll provide a specific timeline during our consultation.

Do you require a deposit?

Yes, we typically require a 50% deposit to start work, with the remaining 50% due upon completion and before the website goes live. This ensures commitment from both parties.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept EFT (Electronic Funds Transfer), credit/debit cards, and PayFast for secure online payments. We also accept installment plans for larger projects.

Do you provide hosting and domain registration?

Yes! We offer complete packages that include hosting, domain registration, and SSL certificates. You can also use your existing hosting if preferred.

Will my website be mobile-friendly?

Absolutely! Every website we build is fully responsive and optimized for all devices - desktop, tablet, and mobile phones.

Do you offer website maintenance?

Yes, we offer monthly maintenance packages starting from R599/month that include updates, backups, security monitoring, and technical support.
